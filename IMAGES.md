# Image credits & replacement guide

All photography in `/assets/images` is **placeholder stock** from Unsplash and Pexels (free licences), graded to one
warm, sun-faded film look with `scripts/grade.py`. Several photos are from the wider Swahili coast (Lamu, Watamu,
Zanzibar) rather than Mombasa itself, and the room/pool photos are **not** of a real Kipwa property.

**Before launch, replace them with the hotel's own photography** (rooms, pool, food, team, the actual beach).
Keep the same filename to swap in place, then run `python scripts/grade.py <raw-photos> assets/images` to match the grade.
